Build your Megamix

m2-megamix is a new program for the Sonic the Fighters arcade board, with fighters and stages from other Sega games. This page builds its ROM set from your own copies of those games, here in your browser: nothing you give it leaves this computer, and this site holds none of Sega's data. The set runs in m2-hle2 (the Play button) or in MAME as sfight.

Everyone who builds the same configuration from the same games gets the same bytes, so a build's link is all a friend needs: they open it, give it their own files, and you can play each other over netplay.
